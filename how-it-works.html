How It Works

Four steps. One Sunday pickup. A full week of dialed-in fuel. Here's exactly what happens from signup to first bite.

🎯

Pick Starter, Athlete, or Elite based on how many meals you want per week. Then choose your exact count at checkout.

2

Every Saturday we cook your meals from scratch using quality ingredients. Exact macros weighed and tracked on every container.

The Details

01. Pick Your Plan

Three tiers built around how serious you are about your nutrition. Whether you're supplementing a few meals or going all-in every day, there's a plan for it.

  • Starter: 6–9 meals at $10.50/meal
  • Athlete: 10–13 meals at $9.50/meal
  • Elite: 14–16 meals at $8.50/meal
  • Cancel or change your plan any time
🎯
Pick Your Tier

02. Choose Your Meals Weekly

Each week you'll get a message with the week's menu. Log in, pick which meals you want, and submit by Friday. You control what goes in your container.

  • Weekly menu sent every Monday
  • Select meals via your personal link
  • Reminder sent Wednesday if not submitted
  • Final reminder Friday before cutoff
📋
Weekly Menu Selection

03. Saturday Prep Day

We don't batch-cook weeks in advance. Every Saturday your meals are prepped from scratch using fresh ingredients. No preservatives, no reheated leftovers.

  • Fresh ingredients sourced weekly
  • Macros measured and labeled on every container
  • Sealed and refrigerated overnight
  • Ready for Sunday pickup
🔥
Fresh Prep Every Saturday

04. Sunday Pickup

Every Sunday, come grab your meals during the pickup window. Your order will be ready, labeled, and packed. Takes 5 minutes. Fuels your whole week.

  • Sunday pickup window: 10am – 2pm
  • Utah location (details in confirmation)
  • In and out in under 5 minutes
  • Meals stay fresh all week refrigerated
🚂
Sunday Pickup
