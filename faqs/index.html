Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Quick answers about how Gainz Train works — signup, weekly meals, pickup, payments, and the rest. If you don't see your question here, text us at (385) 327-8045.

Ordering
How do I sign up?
Go to the Pricing page, pick your tier (Starter, Athlete, or Elite), then choose exactly how many meals per week you want. Click the button and you'll be taken to checkout. Once you're signed up you'll receive a confirmation and your weekly menu selection link.
How do I select my meals each week?
Every Monday you'll get a text with a link to that week's menu. Open it, browse the options, and select your meals. You've got until Saturday night to submit. We'll send a reminder Wednesday if you haven't picked yet, and a final nudge Friday afternoon.
Can I change my meal count or plan?
Yes. You can adjust your plan between billing cycles. Just reach out at gainztrainprep@gmail.com or on Instagram @gainztrainprep and we'll get you sorted.
What happens if I miss the meal selection deadline?
If you don't submit by Saturday night, we'll prep a default selection for you based on that week's menu. You'll still get your full meal count — we won't let you go without fuel. But to get exactly what you want, submit before the cutoff.
Meals & Nutrition
How much protein is in each meal?
Every meal is built to hit 30–50g of protein. We design our menu around protein first, then build the carbs and fats around it. The exact macros are printed on every container.
Are the macros accurate?
Yes. We weigh ingredients during prep and calculate macros per meal. These aren't rough estimates — they're tracked numbers you can log directly into MyFitnessPal or whatever tracker you use.
Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Right now our menu is built around whole food, high-protein options. We don't have dedicated allergen-free prep lines yet. If you have a specific restriction or allergy, contact us before signing up so we can let you know what works for you.
How long do the meals stay fresh?
Meals are prepped fresh Sunday morning and picked up Sunday afternoon. Refrigerated, they stay fresh through the following weekend — typically 5–7 days. No preservatives, so keep them cold and eat within the week.
Are meals gluten-free or dairy-free?
Some meals are, some aren't. Each week's menu will note allergens when applicable. Reach out if you need more detail on a specific meal before ordering.
Pickup & Delivery
Where is pickup?
We're based in Utah County. The exact pickup address is included in your weekly confirmation text.
What are the pickup hours?
Pickup is every Sunday afternoon. You'll get the exact window via text the day before — typically a 2-3 hour window so you can show up whenever works.
Do you offer delivery?
Delivery is launching soon for the Utah County area. Reach out at gainztrainprep@gmail.com if you'd like to be notified when it goes live.
What if I can't make Sunday pickup?
Life happens. Reach out before Sunday and we'll do our best to work something out. The earlier you let us know, the easier it is to figure out an alternative. Text or DM us on Instagram @gainztrainprep.
Payment
When do I get charged?
You're billed weekly on a recurring subscription. Your card is charged at the start of each cycle. You'll receive a receipt for every charge.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel before your next billing cycle and you won't be charged again. Email us at gainztrainprep@gmail.com to cancel.
Do you offer refunds?
If there's an issue with your order — wrong meals, a quality problem — we'll make it right. Reach out within 24 hours of pickup and we'll sort it out. We stand behind the product.
Everything Else
Where are you located?
We're based in Utah County, Utah. Pickup is local — perfect if you train at VASA Fitness or any gym in the Provo/Orem area.
Do you do bulk or group orders?
Interested in setting up your whole gym or team? Reach out. We're open to conversations about group pricing for teams, gyms, or corporate wellness setups. Email gainztrainprep@gmail.com.
Can I give a Gainz Train subscription as a gift?
Not officially set up yet, but we can make it work. Email us at gainztrainprep@gmail.com with the details and we'll sort out a gift arrangement.
