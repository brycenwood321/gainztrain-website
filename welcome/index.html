What Happens Next

Here's How It Works.

1

Weekly Menu Drops

Each week we release the updated menu with that week's meal options. You'll get a notification when it's live.

2

Choose Your Meals

Pick which meals you want for the week before the Friday 11:59 pm MT cutoff. Mix and match based on your goals.

3

We Handle the Rest

If you don't update your order, we'll repeat last week's selection — or substitute the closest option if a meal rotates out.

Weekly Rules

A Few Things to Remember.

  • Update your meal selection by the Friday 11:59 pm MT cutoff to get exactly what you want.
  • No last-minute changes after the Friday 11:59 pm MT cutoff — we're already prepping.
  • If a meal rotates out of the menu, we'll substitute the closest option.
  • Pickup is Sundays. We'll text you the window and location each week.
  • Pause, change your goal, or cancel anytime through your account or by texting us.
