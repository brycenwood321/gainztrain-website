## How Delivery Works

### Just Show Up at Your Front Door.

**1. Sign up & enter your address**
At checkout, you enter your delivery address like any other order. We automatically detect your zone from your zip code.

**2. We confirm your zone by email**
Right after signup, you get an email confirming your zone and weekly delivery fee. If something's off, just reply and we'll fix it.

**3. Pick your meals each week**
Choose your meals at menu.gainztrainprep.com before Saturday cutoff. We prep Sunday morning.

**4. Delivered Sunday afternoon**
We text you a delivery time window the day before. Your meals show up at your door — ready for the week.

### Want to skip the delivery fee?

Pickup at our Orem kitchen is always free. Once you've signed up, reply **PICKUP** to your welcome email and we'll switch you over. You'll get the pickup address and time window the day before.
