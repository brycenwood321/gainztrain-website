Why We Built This

We Were Tired of the Options.

Dry chicken out of a Ziploc. Frozen meals that cost $14 and taste like sadness. Or just... not eating well and wondering why the numbers weren't moving.

We built Gainz Train because we couldn't find what we actually wanted — fresh, high-protein meals with real macros, that didn't require two hours of meal prep on a Sunday, and didn't cost more than a gym membership per week.

So Jayson started making them. For himself, then for friends. Now for 130+ people every week in Utah County.

What We Stand For

The Gainz Train Standard.

A few things we won't compromise on, no matter how fast we grow.

Fresh, Every Week.

We don't freeze and ship. Everything is prepped on Saturday for Sunday pickup. That's not optional — it's the whole point.

Real Macros. No Games.

We publish the actual numbers for every meal. If it doesn't hit the macro bar, it doesn't make the menu.

Affordable by Design.

We want this to be something you can actually sustain. Not a luxury treat, not $15/meal. From $8.50 and competitive on purpose.

Food That Tastes Good.

Performance eating shouldn't mean miserable eating. We care about flavor as much as macros. This is food you actually look forward to.

Built for Consistency.

The best meal plan is the one you stick to. We built the system to make consistency the path of least resistance.

Local, Always.

We're Utah County people, prepping for Utah County people. No warehouses, no national distribution center. Just us, a kitchen, and your Sunday pickup.
