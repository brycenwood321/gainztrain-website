The Process

How It Works.

Simpler than meal prep. More consistent than eating out. Here's exactly what happens each week.

1

Pick Your Plan

Choose the number of meals that fits your lifestyle — anywhere from 6 to 16 meals per week. The more meals, the lower the per-meal cost. Most members land on 10-13 (Athlete tier) because it covers lunch and dinner most days without overdoing it.

→ See Pricing
2

Set Your Goal

After signing up, you'll tell us your goal — Cut, Maintain, or Build. This determines how your meals are portioned. We prep based on goal categories, not custom macros per person. You can update your goal anytime.

3

Choose Meals by Friday

Every week, a new menu drops and you pick which meals you want. Log in at menu.gainztrainprep.com and make your selections before the Friday night cutoff.

If you miss the cutoff, we'll repeat your previous week's selection — or substitute the closest available option if something rotated off the menu.

📅 Cutoff: Friday night each week
4

We Prep Saturday

Everything is prepped fresh on Saturday. Never frozen, never sitting in a warehouse. Real ingredients, cooked the day before you pick them up.

5

Pickup or Delivery Sunday

Grab your meals on Sunday during our pickup window in Utah County. Delivery is also available on Sundays. Exact location and window details will be shared after signup.

🕙 Sundays • Utah County
6

Track Your Macros. Hit Your Goals.

Log your meals in MyFitnessPal or any tracker. Exact protein, carbs, fat, and calories are listed for every meal. No guessing, no estimating. Just results.
